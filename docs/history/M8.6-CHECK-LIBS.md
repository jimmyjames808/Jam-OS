# M8.6 code check: libraries, apps, tools and docs

The last step of [M8.6](../M8.6-PLAN.md#last-the-code-check): a fresh read
of `user/lib` (libos), `user/apps` (libfun and the apps), `user/include`,
`user/tests`, `tools/`, the Makefile and the docs against
[CODING-GUIDE.md](../../CODING-GUIDE.md) and
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md), at main febd124. Findings first;
each one's outcome is filled in as it is fixed.

Severity as in the other reviews: **High** is a program reaching what it
should not, or memory corrupted by input; **Medium** is wrong behaviour a
user will meet, or a test or build that fails for a reason that is not
the code's; **Low** is a wart, a narrow race or a guide rule broken.

What held up (read with untrusted input in mind): `wav.c` (every chunk
read through the callback with its length checked, the data chunk cut to
the file), `mp3.c`'s sniffer (ID3v2 sizes, APE and ID3v1 footers, the
frame walk inside its 8 KiB window), `play_src.c`, `wants.c` (every
offset and note length checked before it is read; a list is printable
ASCII and every line known), `settings.c` (lines bounded, values checked,
the write cut short leaves `settings.new`), `utf8.c` (Table 3-7),
`wallclock.c`, `start.c`'s startup message, `ns.c`'s messages from the
starter, `fsview.c` (with fat's own name check behind it: a backslash or
trailing dot that FatFs would read differently is refused by fat first),
`fs.c`, `spawn.c`, `heap.c`, `printf.c`, jamjar's `id3.c` and
`decoder.c` (the helper's answer is range-checked, its pixels only ever
read with `vmo_read`), and bin/jamcover (sizes checked before stb_image
decodes anything).

## Findings

| # | Sev | Where | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | `Makefile:285-294` (also `:177-180`, `:216-222`) | `make -j8` spins forever (one CPU at 99 %, no output) the first time after a new `abi/idl/*.idl` appears. The copies in `build/driver-include/idl/` are made by a pattern rule, named only as order-only prerequisites, and marked `.SECONDARY`; GNU make 3.81 (the Mac's `/usr/bin/make`) never makes the new one and loops over the graph waiting for it. Reproduced: build at 8edeb93^, check out 8edeb93 (adds `jamcover.idl`), `make -j8`: still spinning after 120 s; `make -d` shows `build/driver-include/idl/jamcover.h` never made and `all` considered 5000 times with no job running. `-j1` finishes. The same pattern guards `build/uinc/jam/*.h` and the FatFs copies. |
| 2 | Medium | `user/apps/fun/pool.c:68-116` | Every pool worker spins about 1 ms (`POOL_SPIN` pauses) after every batch before it sleeps. jamjar runs four or so batches a frame at 60 frames a second, so on the PC's 28 CPUs about 3 CPUs' worth of time goes to spinning while it is open (JAMJAR-REVIEW finding 14). The spin pays only between batches that come back to back (fractal, life); between frames the app is waiting for keys anyway. |
| 3 | Medium | `tools/play-test.sh:146`, `tools/mp3-test.sh:187` | The stopped tone (`long`) must be followed by at least 1 frame of silence, but it is the capture's last sound and the capture ends when the mixer closes the output, which can be right at the fade's last frame: 0 frames is inside the documented 0..period range, and the run fails now and then for it. The check that matters is that the sound faded to nothing before the capture ended. |
| 4 | Low | `tools/checkdocs.py:33` | docs/history/ is skipped entirely. Its spans rightly keep the paths of their time, but its links are navigation and can rot unseen (all of them work today). Decision: check links and anchors there, not spans. |
| 5 | Low | `user/tests/utest/music.c:14-15` | `#include`s the player's `spectrum.c` and `tracks.c` (the guide: never `#include` a `.c` file; JAMJAR-REVIEW finding 18). Everything it uses is public in `music.h`: the Makefile can link the player's two objects into utest instead. |
| 6 | Low | `user/apps/fun/scale.c:12-36` | `box()` nests five levels (the guide: three). |
| 7 | Low | `user/apps/fun/pool.c:127-136` | `pool_start` leaks a worker's stack and event when the event or the thread can't be made. |

| 9 | Low | `docs/TESTING.md` "The other tools" | `tools/checkwants.py` (run by every build that packs the boot image) is not listed. |
| 10 | Low | `user/lib/ns.c:497-532` | `svc_get` returns a kept handle after dropping `kept_lock`; another thread that finds that service dead closes it, and the handle number can be reused by an unrelated handle before the first thread calls on it. Narrow (a service must die while two threads use it). |
| 11 | Low | `user/lib/heap.c:38-43`, `ns.c:52-56` | The heap's lock spins with `pause` and never yields: a holder preempted on a busy CPU leaves the waiters spinning for its time slice. ns.c's locks sleep 20 us between tries. Narrow; noted with JAMJAR-REVIEW finding 13. |
