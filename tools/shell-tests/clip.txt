# Copy and paste between two terminal windows (docs/G1-PLAN.md, "As built:
# copy and paste"), on a plain boot with a USB keyboard and mouse (the
# compositor's): terminal 1 prints a command; a triple click on that line
# (QEMU's mouse through the monitor) selects it, drawn on the blackcurrant
# tint (`clip-selected`); Super+C copies it; `term` opens terminal 2, whose
# window takes the keys; Super+V there pastes it onto the shell's line,
# bracketed, so nothing runs (`clip-pasted`) until Enter, which runs it.
# Then a two-line selection in terminal 1 (a press, a drag down a line, a
# release: `clip-drag`), copied and pasted with Ctrl+Shift+V into terminal
# 3, arrives as one line, its line break a space, and runs only at Enter. Pixel positions are for OVMF's 1280x800, the first window
# alone in its tile: the text starts at (18, 58), cells 9x21.
# Run: tools/clip-test.sh <outdir> (it checks the screenshot's tint too).
wait 120 Jam OS shell
wait {prompt}
# the boot's last log lines in first, so the text starts where it should
sleep 4
send clear
wait {prompt}
send echo "echo ""pas""ted-""ok"
wait 10 echo pasted-ok
wait {prompt}
sleep 1
# row 1 (the output line), column 2: a triple click
pointer 40 89
monitor mouse_button 1
monitor mouse_button 0
monitor mouse_button 1
monitor mouse_button 0
monitor mouse_button 1
monitor mouse_button 0
sleep 1
shot clip-selected
monitor sendkey meta_l-c
wait 10 console: terminal 1: copied 14 bytes

send term
wait 10 terminal 2 opens
wait 30 Jam OS shell
wait 10 {prompt}
sleep 1
monitor sendkey meta_l-v
wait 10 console: terminal 2: pasted 14 bytes (bracketed)
wait 10 {prompt} echo pasted-ok
# (sendkey holds its keys 100 ms: Super let go before Enter, which with
# Super would open a terminal)
sleep 1
shot clip-pasted
send
wait 10 pasted-ok
wait 10 {prompt}

# two lines of terminal 1 (the left tile now): a press on row 0's first
# "e" (which gives terminal 1 the keys), a drag to row 1's column 10, and
# Super+C; `term` there opens terminal 3, where Ctrl+Shift+V pastes it:
# one line, its line break a space, run by Enter
pointer 85 68
monitor mouse_button 1
monitor mouse_move 6 6
monitor mouse_move 6 6
monitor mouse_move 6 6
monitor mouse_move 6 6
monitor mouse_button 0
sleep 1
shot clip-drag
monitor sendkey meta_l-c
wait 10 console: terminal 1: copied
send term
wait 10 terminal 3 opens
wait 30 Jam OS shell
wait 10 {prompt}
sleep 1
monitor sendkey ctrl-shift-v
wait 10 console: terminal 3: pasted
wait 10 {prompt} echo "echo ""pas""ted-""ok" echo pas
# sendkey holds its keys 100 ms: Ctrl and Shift let go before typing on
sleep 1
send
wait 10 echo pasted-ok echo pas
wait 10 {prompt}

send reboot -f
wait reboot: resetting
