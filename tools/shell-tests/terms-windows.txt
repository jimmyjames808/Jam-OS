# Terminals as windows (docs/G1-PLAN.md, track T1) on a plain boot, the
# compositor's (serialin and the keyboards are its input sources, not the
# console's): the first terminal opens as a window; `term` opens terminal 2,
# whose window takes the keys (a client's first window does), so `exit`
# typed now ends terminal 2's shell and closes it, and the keys come back
# to the first. Every terminal is equal (the polish track): `exit` in the
# first closes it too, and with none left the desktop shows its wallpaper
# and top bar (terms-none); Super+Enter on a keyboard opens terminal 1
# again (the lowest number free), and another Super+Enter terminal 2. The
# compositor killed, both windows come back. Screenshots along the way
# (terms-one, terms-two, terms-none, terms-back).
# Run: QEMU_INPUT=tools/shell-tests/terms-windows.txt \
#      QEMU_USB="-device usb-kbd,bus=xhci.0,port=2" tools/qemu-test.sh build/test terms-windows shell
wait 120 Jam OS shell
seen 10 console: window mode: terminal 1 opens a
wait {prompt}
sleep 2
shot terms-one
send term
wait 10 terminal 2 opens
wait 30 Jam OS shell
wait 10 {prompt}
seen 30 console: window mode: terminal 2 opens a
sleep 2
shot terms-two
send exit
wait 10 init: terminal 2: its shell ended with exit
wait 10 init: terminal 2 is closed
sleep 1

# the first terminal closes like any other
send exit
wait 10 init: terminal 1: its shell ended with exit
wait 10 init: terminal 1 is closed
wait 10 init: no terminal is open
sleep 2
shot terms-none
monitor sendkey meta_l-ret
wait 10 init: terminal 1 opens
wait 30 Jam OS shell
wait 10 {prompt}
seen 30 console: window mode: terminal 1 opens a
sleep 1
send echo "ok-""again"
wait 10 ok-again
wait {prompt}

monitor sendkey meta_l-ret
wait 10 init: terminal 2 opens
wait 30 Jam OS shell
wait 10 {prompt}
sleep 1
send kill compositor
wait 10 init: bin/compositor was killed
wait 30 console: connected again (connection 2)
wait 30 console: connected again (connection 2)
sleep 3
shot terms-back
send echo "ok-""back"
wait 10 ok-back

send reboot -f
wait reboot: resetting
