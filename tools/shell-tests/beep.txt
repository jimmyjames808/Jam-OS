# `beep` and `hda gain` (drivers/hda, the shell's cmd/beep.c and
# cmd/hda.c): one emulated controller with QEMU's hda-output codec writing
# what it plays to a WAV file; use tools/beep-test.sh (it adds the devices
# and checks the log, the codec's verbs and the file). One 440 Hz tone of
# 500 ms at the default gain; then the gain shown, set, and clamped at
# both ends of the amp's range. (The markers are echoed as bp-""1 so the
# typed line itself never matches the wait.)
wait 120 Jam OS shell
wait {prompt}
seen 60 1 codec(s) answered; 0 verb(s) timed out
send hda gain; echo bp-""1
wait hda: gain -30.0 dB (step 44; -74.0 to 0.0 dB)
wait bp-1
wait {prompt}
send beep 440 500; echo bp-""2
wait 30 beep: 440 Hz for 500 ms at -30.0 dB
wait bp-2
wait {prompt}
send hda gain -20.4; echo bp-""3
wait hda: gain -20.0 dB (step 54;
wait bp-3
wait {prompt}
send hda gain -500; echo bp-""4
wait hda: gain -74.0 dB (step 0;
wait bp-4
wait {prompt}
send hda gain 6; echo bp-""5
wait hda: gain 0.0 dB (step 74;
wait bp-5
wait {prompt}
send hda gain -30; hda | grep "hda: gain"; echo bp-""6
wait hda: gain -30.0 dB (step 44;
wait bp-6
wait {prompt}
send beep 30000; echo bp-""7
wait usage: beep [hz] [ms]
wait bp-7
wait {prompt}
send reboot -f
wait reboot: resetting
