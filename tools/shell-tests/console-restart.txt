# A console started again late in a boot draws only the last 256 KiB of
# the kernel log (console main.c, CATCH_UP_DRAWN), not the whole 4 MiB
# ring: eight rounds of the klog gap test put about 550 KiB of filler into
# the log, then `kill console`; the new console says what it drew, and the
# shell comes back on it.
# The filler overflows COM1's 64 KiB transmit ring (kernel/dev/serial.c drops
# what doesn't fit: the text is still in the log and on the screen), and the
# shell's answer and prompt come at once after it, while the ring is still
# full: they are on the screen but cut from the serial line, so a wait for
# the prompt here waited for nothing (the PC under load, where QEMU drains
# the ring slowly). The echo comes after the ring has drained.
# Run: QEMU_INPUT=tools/shell-tests/console-restart.txt tools/qemu-test.sh build/test console-restart shell
wait 120 Jam OS shell
wait {prompt}
send ktest console_klog_read_after_gap loops=8; sleep 5; echo ok-$?-flood
wait 120 ok-0-flood
wait {prompt}

wait {prompt}
