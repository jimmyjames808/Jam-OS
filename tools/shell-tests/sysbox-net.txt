# `net`, `ping` and `host` as programs (user/sysbox, sys-net: bin/net, bin/ping,
# bin/host; their list is `svc net` and `svc dns`) on a network: tools/ping-test.sh
# runs this after net.txt, on the same stick (net.address set) and the peer
# (tools/netpeer.py answers ARP and ICMP echo for any address on VLAN 21).
# The program says what the builtin says (the first three lines of `net`: the
# fourth counts the programs that have the service open, and the shell's
# counts differ), pings through the gateway, ends with its summary when Ctrl+C
# (SIGINT, caught) stops it, and refuses a name with no resolver answering.
