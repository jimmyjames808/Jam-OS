# Review: the shell's `kill` reaches every process in the system
# (debug_command's scope is the ROOT of the caller's job tree). Killing
# init would end it for good (the kernel prints its RESULTS box, "root job
# ... LEAKED", while the console and shell run on with nobody to restart
# them), so the shell refuses it. The services init supervises may go:
# serialin comes back, and devmgr comes back with its drivers.
wait 120 Jam OS shell
wait jam>
send kill init
wait 10 not killing it
wait jam>
send kill devmgr
# (init's line goes to the kernel log, the shell's through the console: their
# order on the serial port isn't fixed, hence `seen`)
wait 10 shell: killed process
seen 30 init: devmgr and its drivers are gone
wait 60 init: devmgr:
send kill serialin
wait bin/serialin was killed
sleep 1
send help
wait Commands:
wait jam>
send reboot
wait reboot: resetting
