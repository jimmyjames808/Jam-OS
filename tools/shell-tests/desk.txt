# The desktop's plumbing (track D2b; tools/desk-test.sh runs this): a plain
# boot with a usb-kbd on xhci port 2, a usb-mouse on port 3 and QEMU's
# e1000e with the network peer (DHCP: 10.2.21.100). Clicks are at OVMF's
# 1280x800.
#   - netstack's "Connected" notice, with the peer's lease;
#   - `notify -w` posts a card with buttons and waits; a click on its first
#     button (the card's place: notify.c, under the 40-pixel strip at
#     y 48, 300 wide from 10 off the right edge; a body's line, then the
#     buttons) answers it;
#   - the terminal's text cursor shape (a screenshot only);
#   - init launches only the desktop's apps (`launch`);
#   - the search box (Super tapped alone): "jamj" and Enter starts Jamjar
#     through init, the cursor busy until its window is up; Super+Q closes
#     it and init sees it end;
#   - its "Run ... in a terminal" row: terminal 2 opens and its shell runs
#     the line (seq's 7771 is the command's output, not the typed line).
wait 120 Jam OS shell
wait {prompt}
seen 30 compositor: keyboard 0627:0001 ready
seen 60 netstack: link up
wait 60 Connected: 10.2.21.100
# ---- a notice and its button --------------------------------------------------------------
send notify -b Yes -b No -w Tea The kettle is on
wait 10 posted
sleep 0.6
shot desk-notice
pointer 1028 112
monitor mouse_button 1
sleep 0.2
monitor mouse_button 0
wait 10 : Yes
wait {prompt}
# the pointer over the terminal's text: the text bar it asked for
# (wp-cursor-shape-v1); the arrow over its padding
pointer 400 400
sleep 0.5
shot desk-text-cursor
# ---- what init launches ---------------------------------------------------------------------
send launch fractal
wait 10 launch: fractal is not one of the desktop's apps
wait {prompt}
send launch ../bin/shell
wait 10 launch: init: not a valid name
wait {prompt}
# ---- the search box: Jamjar ----------------------------------------------------------------
monitor sendkey meta_l
sleep 0.5
usbkeys jamj
monitor sendkey ret
wait 20 init: launch jamjar: OK
wait 10 compositor: launched jamjar
wait 30 compositor: Jamjar's window is up: the cursor is not busy
sleep 1
shot desk-jamjar
monitor sendkey meta_l-q
wait 20 init: jamjar ended
# ---- the search box: a command in a new terminal ---------------------------------------------
monitor sendkey meta_l
sleep 0.5
usbkeys seq 7770 7772
monitor sendkey ret
wait 10 init: terminal 2 opens to run "seq 7770 7772"
wait 60 7771
seen 30 compositor: Terminal 2's window is up: the cursor is not busy
sleep 1
shot desk-run
send reboot -f
wait 30 reboot: resetting
